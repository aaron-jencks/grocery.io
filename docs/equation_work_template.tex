\section*{How to Use This File}
Replace the sample equations below with your own work.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Use \texttt{equation} for one numbered equation.
  \item Use \texttt{align} to show multiple steps with numbers.
  \item Use \texttt{align*} if you want steps without numbers.
  \item Add \verb|\label{...}| and refer to equations with \verb|\eqref{...}|.
\end{itemize}

\section{Equation List}

\subsection{Single Equation}
This is a single numbered equation:

\begin{equation}
E = mc^2
\label{eq:energy}
\end{equation}

You can refer back to Equation~\eqref{eq:energy}.

\subsection{Show Your Work}
Use this format when you want to show each algebra step:

\begin{align}
2x + 3 &= 11 \label{eq:start} \\
2x &= 8 \label{eq:subtract3} \\
x &= 4 \label{eq:solution}
\end{align}

Equation~\eqref{eq:solution} gives the final answer.

\subsection{System of Equations}

\begin{align}

y &= x + 5
\end{align}

\subsection{Unnumbered Scratch Work}
Use \texttt{align*} if you do not want equation numbers:

\begin{align*}
(x+1)^2 &= x^2 + 2x + 1 \\
         &= x(x+2) + 1
\end{align*}
